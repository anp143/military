Military Asset Management System (MAMS)

The MAMS is a secure, intelligent, and role-based solution designed to help commanders and logistics teams effectively manage assets.

Monitor Opening and Closing Balances, record Purchases, Transfers, Assignments, and Expenditures, and maintain complete visibility over operations across multiple bases.

Core Features

  • 📊 Comprehensive dashboard with key asset metrics and visual summaries
  • 🔄 Real-time tracking of purchases, transfers, and asset movements
  • 🎯 Assignment and expenditure tracking for efficient utilization
  • 🛡️ Role-based access control ensuring secure and tiered access
  • 🧾 Complete transaction logging for auditing and transparency
